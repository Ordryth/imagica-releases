# Imagica Desktop: Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 November 2026**

Imagica Desktop ("Imagica", "the app") is made by **Ordryth Pvt Limited** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information on Windows, macOS and Linux, including the Microsoft Store version. It applies to Imagica Desktop version 0.1.30 and later.

## In short

- **Your pictures are made on your own computer.** Your prompts, pictures and settings stay on your computer. We don't receive them.
- **We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.**
- The app connects to the internet only to download what it needs (the AI engine, AI models, updates), and for the optional features you choose to use, all listed below.

## What stays on your computer

These are stored only on your computer, in Imagica's own folders. We never receive a copy:

- the prompts you type and the pictures you make or edit (your gallery and history)
- photos you add to create a character
- the AI models you download
- your settings, including any Hugging Face or Civitai access token you add

They stay until you delete them in the app, or remove Imagica from your computer.

## When the app uses the internet

**1. Downloading the AI engine and AI models.** On first start and when you choose a model, the app downloads files from these services:

- **GitHub:** the AI engine and app updates, from our public releases page.
- **python.org, python-build-standalone on GitHub, download.pytorch.org, pypi.org and bootstrap.pypa.io:** the Python runtime and libraries the engine runs on.
- **Hugging Face, Civitai and Meta (dl.fbaipublicfiles.com):** the AI models and tools you choose, and the small AI prompt helper the first time you use "Improve prompt".

Like any download, these services receive standard connection details such as your IP address. If you added a Hugging Face or Civitai token, it is sent only to that service, to download models that need it. When you search Hugging Face or Civitai inside the app, your search words are sent to that service.

**2. Checking for updates.** The app asks GitHub for the latest version number. If you installed Imagica from the Microsoft Store, updates come from the Store instead.

**3. Fonts.** The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives standard connection details such as your IP address.

**4. Optional sign-in with Google.** You can use Imagica without an account. If you choose to sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication (a Google service) process your name, email address and profile picture to sign you in. The app keeps them on your computer to show your account, and loads your profile picture from Google. Without a profile picture, the app draws a letter avatar on your computer; your email address is not sent anywhere for it. We don't use your account details for advertising or marketing.

**5. Anonymous usage statistics (switched off).** The app includes support for anonymous usage statistics through Aptabase, but **it is currently switched off and nothing is sent**. If a future version switches it on, it would send only an event name (for example "app started"), the app version, your system name and version, your language and a random number that changes every time the app starts. It would never send names, email addresses, prompts, pictures or file names, and you could turn it off at any time in **Settings > Share anonymous usage statistics**.

**6. Problem reports, only when you click "Report problem".** This opens a pre-filled problem report on our public GitHub page. **Nothing is sent unless you choose to submit it there**, and you can read and edit the whole text first. The report contains the app version, your system, your computer's graphics card, memory and processor, the error message and the last lines of the AI engine's log. The app removes user names from file paths, prompts and access tokens from the report. Reports submitted on GitHub are public and fall under GitHub's own privacy statement.

## What the AI does

All AI processing (making and editing pictures, and the "Improve prompt" helper) runs on your own computer. Your prompts and pictures are not sent to us or to any AI service.

## Children

Imagica is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices

- Use Imagica without signing in.
- Delete any picture, model or setting in the app, or remove the app to delete everything it stored.
- Don't add access tokens if you don't download models that need them.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
